🎛️ Compression Visualizer
A real-time, browser-based DSP compressor designed to help producers visually understand how Attack, Release, Threshold, and Ratio affect an audio waveform.
Unlike standard browser audio nodes, this tool uses custom mathematics to provide true downward compression without forced auto-makeup gain, so you can see exactly how the transient is physically squashed.

🚀 Quick Start
Click Choose File and upload a punchy drum loop or kick sample (.wav or .mp3).
Hit Power / Play (or press the Spacebar).
Pull down the Threshold and push up the Ratio.
Adjust the Attack and Release knobs and watch the green compressed waveform change shape against the original gray waveform in real-time.

🎮 Controls & Navigation
Play / Pause: Spacebar
Seek: Click anywhere on the waveform screen to jump to that moment.
Loop a Region: Click & Drag across the screen to highlight a specific drum hit. (Click outside the green box to reset).
Horizontal Zoom (Time): Mouse Wheel to zoom in on a single transient.
Vertical Zoom (Amplitude): Ctrl + Mouse Wheel to make the waveforms taller.

👁️ What You Are Seeing
Gray Waveform: Your original, uncompressed audio.
Green Waveform: The heavily compressed signal.
Analog GR Meter: A ballistic visual representation of your gain reduction in decibels (dB).
